about

A little about me

I’m a Computer Science undergraduate at the Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology (SLIIT), with a growing focus on cybersecurity and web development.

On the security side, I work with Kali Linux and Arch Linux, using tools like Wifite for wireless security testing and OSINT techniques for reconnaissance and information gathering. On the development side, I build responsive front-ends with Bootstrap and Node.js — and take on client work through Code.lowa.

I enjoy the balance between offensive security thinking — understanding how systems break — and constructive development — building things that work well and look good. I’m always expanding my skills through coursework, self-study and hands-on projects, and always open to connecting with others in the tech and infosec community.
